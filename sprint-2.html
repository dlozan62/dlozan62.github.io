Assignment 2 · September 28, 2026

Estimation Activity 2

Our selected slice covers student access to dining locations, hours, menus, sold-out items, dietary filtering, and mobile viewing. The six stories total 22 points.

The estimate is 270 to 560 team hours, with an expected value of about 438 hours. At an assumed $60/hour, the cost range is $16,200 to $33,600. With 4 people working an assumed 30 productive hours each per week, the range is 2.3 to 4.7 weeks.
