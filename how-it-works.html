The DineCheck approach · Planned workflow

Check first.
Walk with a plan.

DineCheck proposes to bring dining hours, menu availability, and update times into one place so students can decide where to eat before crossing campus.

This page describes the proposed system. Live campus data and staff integration are still to be validated.

For students

Three steps to a more informed choice.

  1. 01 · Find

    Check what's open

    Review a dining location's hours and service status before leaving. An open building may have different kitchen hours.

  2. 02 · Compare

    Look for your meal

    Check which menu items are available and review dietary information. Confirm allergens and suitability with dining staff.

  3. 03 · Decide

    Check the update time

    Use the last update time to judge how current the information is, then choose a location or plan a backup.

For dining staff

A small update that helps the next student.

Update service status

Record changes to hours or service so students can see when a kitchen closes or a location stops serving.

Keep availability current

Mark an item unavailable when it sells out and available again when it returns to the menu.

Show when it changed

Pair each update with a visible timestamp so students can assess the freshness of the information.

The next questions

What the team still needs to validate.

Reliable information

Confirm access to campus dining data and define how outdated or missing information should appear.

A practical staff workflow

Determine who can maintain updates and whether the process fits a busy dining shift.

Clear student decisions

Evaluate whether students understand the status information and can use it to choose where to eat.
