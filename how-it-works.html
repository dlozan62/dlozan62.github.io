Our plan for DineCheck

Check before you walk.

Our idea is to put dining hours, item availability, and update times in one place so students can choose where to eat before leaving.

This is how we plan for DineCheck to work. It isn’t connected to live campus dining data yet.

For students

How students would use it.

  1. 01 · Find

    Check what's open

    Review a dining location's hours and service status before leaving. An open building may have different kitchen hours.

  2. 02 · Compare

    Look for your meal

    Check which menu items are available and review dietary information. Confirm allergens and suitability with dining staff.

  3. 03 · Decide

    Check the update time

    Use the last update time to judge how current the information is, then choose a location or plan a backup.

For dining staff

How staff would keep it updated.

Update service status

Record changes to hours or service so students can see when a kitchen closes or a location stops serving.

Keep availability current

Mark an item unavailable when it sells out and available again when it returns to the menu.

Show when it changed

Show when each status was last updated so students can tell how old the information is.

The next questions

What we still need to test.

Reliable information

Find out what dining data we can access and how to label missing or outdated information.

A practical staff workflow

Find out who would post updates and whether they could keep up during a busy shift.

Clear student decisions

Test whether students understand the statuses and find them useful when choosing where to eat.
