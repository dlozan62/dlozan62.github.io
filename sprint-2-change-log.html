Change log

This record documents the change from the flexible meal-plan direction to the Campus Dining Availability System.

Download matching PDF

Project direction

What changed: The project moved from a flexible campus meal plan to current item, station, kitchen, counter, and service information.

Why: Twelve focused availability interviews documented cases where students learned about a change only after arriving. Reported consequences included detours, delays, substitutions, hunger, and extra spending.

Downstream effect: The project title, problem statement, business objective, scope, assumptions, stakeholders, risks, requirements, prototype plan, and success criteria were revised.

Evidence accounting

What changed: The team separated 12 focused availability interviews from 13 meal-plan preference responses in the 25-response packet.

Why: The meal-plan responses provide background for the Sprint 1 direction but do not validate the availability system.

Downstream effect: Research totals, page labels, charter evidence, and future interview targets now distinguish the two groups.

Scope and delivery

What changed: This phase now covers validation, requirements, prototype development, usability testing, and feasibility review.

Why: The team has not established a live data source, update owner, or approved university and vendor integration path.

Downstream effect: Production deployment, live integrations, notifications, user accounts, payment changes, and campus-wide rollout remain outside the current scope.

Open Sprint 2 work

What changed: The portal now marks Sprint 2 as in progress instead of presenting the research packet as the complete sprint deliverable.

Why: The required business case, estimation appendix, ROI analysis, and team-authored go/no-go recommendation are not complete.

Downstream effect: Sprint 2 will remain in progress until the team supplies the required documents and matching PDFs.
