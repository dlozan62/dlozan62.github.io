Sprint 1 · Research and charter · Complete

Getting Started with the Project...

We started out working on meal-plan preferences. A later round of research turned up a more immediate problem: students sometimes find out that food or a service is unavailable only after they reach a dining location. Sprint 1 records that change in direction and the charter for the next round of validation.

Research Timeline

See the results of our research

  1. Earlier Direction

    Meal-Plan Preferences

    Thirteen response sheets explored plan flexibility. Eight participants preferred a dollar balance and five preferred set meals. One respondent was staff. These small counts describe this packet only.

  2. Focused Research Round

    Availability Experiences

    Twelve interviews asked about actual dining decisions and the effect of sold-out items, closed services, and information that had not been updated.

  3. Current Direction

    Test a Dining Availability System

    The team compared five candidate ideas and selected availability as the problem to validate further. The present charter covers research, requirements, and prototype evaluation.
