Sprint 1: our first idea

We first explored flexible meal plans

We began by asking students about meal-plan cost, flexibility, unused money, and food choices. This page keeps that early work so you can see where the project started. In Sprint 2, we changed the project after hearing about a more immediate problem: food and services that were unavailable without warning.

What we decided

Our first choice was a flexible meal plan

The team selected a flexible campus meal-plan idea for students with different budgets and eating habits. The proposed direction included more than one plan structure, rollover for unused value, and monthly or semester payment options.

We kept this page as a record of our first decision. The Sprint 2 change log explains why later interviews moved the project toward dining availability.
