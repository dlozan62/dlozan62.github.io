Starter · Step 4

What we took from the comparison

Hidden work

T (Testing) and U (Usability and accessibility) each appear in all five rows. Most of the products we have been involved with required thorough testing of features or workflows.

Time and team size

Usually a rushed 3 months, with a group of 2 to 3 team members. This suggests that, while feasible within the time scale of this class, a first release would still require a large number of work hours to reach a first release candidate.

What we will copy and avoid

We will copy business information such as open hours and options like gluten-free so people can plan where they want to visit and not waste their time. A mistake we will avoid is thinking too small about the scale, which could lead to a rebuild and outages.

Our first rough estimate

Our comparables suggest a first release would take 2 to 3 months with a team of 4.

The 3-month experience and the 2-to-3-month release estimate are the team's starter answers. The comparable-product table does not establish a shared 3-month development timeline. Activity 2 estimates a smaller slice using function points and use-case points.
