The problem

Students sometimes reach a dining location and find that an item, station, kitchen, or service is unavailable.

We want to give students a simple way to check what is actually available before they walk to a dining location. This could help them avoid a wasted trip, a long wait, or spending more than planned.

Current research question

What happens when students cannot trust campus dining information?

We are studying whether menus and hours are accurate, when food is unavailable, how allergies and dietary needs affect choices, and what students do when the information is wrong.
