Proposed support hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m., America/Denver. Timers begin at detection during those hours, or at the next support opening for reports received outside them. Response means acknowledging the incident and starting investigation; it is not a guaranteed resolution time.

3.2 How escalation works

The person who notices the issue records the URL, detection time, symptoms, and supporting evidence, then alerts Zachary in the team chat. Zachary assigns severity and a fixer. If Jacob cannot begin within the response target, Zachary assigns Daniel. If Zachary is unavailable, Daniel acts as backup commander and delegates fixing to Jacob. Only assigned fixers change the site during an incident. The communicator works from the shared incident log so updates agree. Close the incident after the affected checks pass and the commander confirms access is restored. Record any remaining budget restriction separately.

Review trigger: Create or update an incident review for Severity 1 or 2 incidents, an exhausted error budget, or a problem that exposes a gap in checking or publishing.

02 · Reliability planning

Error budgets and policy

Error Budgets: Two-Week Period.

Availability budget

14 x 24 x 60 = 20,160 minutes. At 0.5% allowed failure, the budget is 100.8 minutes (1 hour, 40 minutes, 48 seconds). With whole one-minute checks, no more than 100 failed checks are allowed to remain at or above 99.5%.

Download budget

floor(total document checks x 0.01) failed checks. For example, 15 documents checked daily for 14 days produce 210 checks and allow 2 failures. Use the actual check count; this example is not a measured total.

For either budget

At 50% used, prioritize reliability work and review upcoming changes. At exhaustion, pause feature releases and fix the affected service. Reliability fixes may continue. Resume feature releases only after the fix is verified and the affected target is met again over the rolling 14-day window.

Decision owner and notification

Decision owner: Zachary, as incident commander, applies the policy using the logs; Jacob supplies download results.

Notification: Salvador informs the team and instructor within 4 support hours of budget exhaustion, explains the impact and recovery plan, and sends a daily update until recovery.

Link to delivery (DORA) metrics

If either budget is exhausted, the next sprint publishes only through reviewed pull requests that pass the validator, accepting lower deployment frequency and longer lead time for a lower change fail rate. Delivery metrics describe the team and are not used in peer evaluation.

The link to delivery metrics is section 2.4 of the November 2 Service Levels revision; the rest of this section matches the Error Budgets document.

03 · Incident analysis

Blameless incident review: Sprint 3 document download links

Section 4 of our Service Levels document, in full.

Evidence-based draft. The repository records the link defect and repair. The actual detection time, live exposure duration, and number of affected users are not recorded in the reviewed evidence. Confirm these details from team records before publishing.

1. Summary

Sprint 3 source contained download URLs with percent-encoded Liquid template expressions. Commit 6596c7b, titled “Fix Sprint 3 PDF downloads,” replaced these with valid PDF links, added PDF files, and expanded validation. The issue is reviewed as a publishing and validation gap.

2. Impact

The malformed links could prevent visitors from downloading Sprint 3 records. The reviewed source confirms defective URLs; it does not establish which deployed versions users encountered or how many download attempts failed. No data loss is established by the reviewed evidence.

3. Timeline

Incident timeline
StageDetails
Before repairSprint 3 source contained encoded template expressions in document links.
DetectionExact time and reporting method are not recorded in the reviewed evidence.
RepairCommit 6596c7b corrected download URLs, added PDF assets, and strengthened checks and tests.
Live recoveryDeployment and recovery timestamps must be confirmed from the deployment history; a source commit alone does not confirm restoration.

4. Contributing causes

The publishing process allowed encoded template syntax into link attributes. Existing validation did not reject this pattern before the repair. A final download check was needed to verify that generated links resolved to the intended files. The validator does not block publishing, because the branch-based GitHub Pages build deploys on every push (to verify in Settings > Pages > Source).

5. What went well

Version history preserved the defect and repair for review. The fix addressed both the links and the validation gap. Added tests cover empty URLs and unresolved or encoded template expressions, reducing the chance of the same defect recurring.

Interface sketch
