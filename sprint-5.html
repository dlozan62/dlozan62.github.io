Outside-user review

Try the flow. Tell us where you hesitate.

  1. Find the University of Texas at El Paso and open the campus dining list.
  2. Find an open venue and identify where you would check its hours.
  3. Select Vegan and avoid Milk in the dining list. Find a matching fictional item, then compare its ingredients and stock state.
  4. Try Halal to review the no-match state. Clear the filters and compare in-stock and out-of-stock items.
  5. Open Dining Option #2. What tells you that the venue is closed?
  6. Switch to Maps and choose a venue using its numbered pin.
  7. Preview location unavailable. Can you still find dining information?
  8. Change campus and search for a name with no results. What would you do next?

For each task, record whether it was completed independently, where the reviewer hesitated, and their exact comments. Ask whether they preferred the list or map and what information was missing. These are planned review tasks; no completed sessions or findings are claimed here.

Wireframe updates

What this version improves.

  • Dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal), nine allergen exclusions, matching item counts, and an in-stock filter.
  • Unknown dietary or allergen information is excluded when a relevant filter is active. Sample matches are not allergy-safety guarantees; confirm ingredients and cross-contact with staff.
  • Responsive phone layouts, larger reading text, and clearer touch targets.
  • Working Dining, Maps, and Search navigation with visible keyboard focus.
  • Venue-specific menu headings and closed-state behavior from dining rows and map pins.
  • Search empty-state feedback and a reversible location-unavailable preview.
  • All four annotated screens remain visible for comparison and printing.

Next: conduct outside-user reviews, record observations, and revise the flow based on evidence. Live campus feeds, actual location services, and verified menu details remain future work.
